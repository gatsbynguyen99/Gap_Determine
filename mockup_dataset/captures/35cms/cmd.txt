Gap calculation 
1. Disparity Edges 
    Stereo camera captures disparity - horizontal pixel shift between left and right images. 
    Disparity Edges means depth discontinuities, when depth ubruptly chnages between neigbouing pixels. 
    Why Disparity edge detection is better than RGB edge detection - because sensitive to shadows, paint, rust, shine, needs good contrast.

    Disparity = Raw Geometry
    Depth = post-processed and smoothed using Disparity
    We get clearer edges in disparity

    Select vertical edges (pipe sides)
   ↓
    Measure pixel gap
